The Green Room

An ongoing speaker series built for the Australian Greens, drawing leading voices from the progressive and global left.

Client

The Australian Greens

Engine

Commercial

Scope

Created and produced end to end

Status

Ongoing series

The situation

The Australian Greens wanted a speaker series that built profile and engaged supporters without draining the budget.

What we did

Commercial

We created and produce The Green Room end to end, format, booking, production and commercial model. The series has featured Owen Jones and Gary Stevenson, with two further editions in October headlined by Caroline Lucas and Abby Martin, and more leading figures from the global left in discussion.

The result

A large, engaged live audience and strong revenue from its first edition.

The unexpected result was its value beyond fundraising: the series became a genuine brand and profile asset for the party, not just a revenue line.

What it proves

A self-funding event that also elevates the organisation behind it. The commercial engine working in service of the mission.
